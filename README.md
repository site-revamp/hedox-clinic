Hedox Clinic – Homepage Redesign
1. Website Overview
Hedox Clinic is a doctor-led medical aesthetics clinic operated by Hedox Ltd, providing non-surgical cosmetic treatments, advanced skin rejuvenation and professional clinical skincare in London and Twickenham.

The redesigned homepage focuses on presenting Hedox as a medically led aesthetics practice, highlighting its experienced practitioners, treatment expertise and personalised approach to natural-looking results. The design aims to help prospective patients understand the clinic's services, establish confidence in its medical expertise and access treatment and appointment information more easily.
2. Main Improvements
1. Stronger Medical Positioning and Professional Credibility
The homepage gives greater prominence to the clinic's doctor-led approach, founder Dr Stephen Humble and the wider medical team. Professional qualifications and clinical expertise are presented more clearly to strengthen the clinic's specialist positioning and distinguish it from generic beauty and aesthetics providers.
2. Clearer Treatment Organisation and Service Presentation
The range of aesthetic treatments, including anti-wrinkle injections, dermal fillers, microneedling, polynucleotides and skin rejuvenation, is organised into clearer sections. This improves the presentation of the clinic's services and makes it easier for prospective patients to identify relevant treatments.
3. Improved Appointment Journey and Clinic Information
Appointment and contact information receives clearer presentation, with better visibility of the London and Twickenham clinic locations. The redesign makes key contact details and appointment pathways easier to identify, helping prospective patients move from exploring treatments to making an enquiry.
3. Design Improvements
- Visual Presentation: A refined medical aesthetics appearance supports Hedox Clinic's premium, doctor-led positioning.
- Layout: A clearer content hierarchy separates the clinic introduction, medical team, treatments and appointment information.
- Typography: Consistent typography creates a more polished and readable presentation.
- User Experience: Improved treatment organisation and more accessible appointment information simplify navigation.
- Content Consistency: Medical credentials, treatment descriptions and clinic information follow a more cohesive presentation.
- Overall Appearance: The redesigned homepage establishes a more professional and unified visual identity while maintaining focus on clinical expertise and natural-looking aesthetic results.
